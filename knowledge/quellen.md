## Notes on individual sources

The Digital Omnibus PDF is the Official Journal version, downloaded by hand because EUR-Lex blocks scripted access.

The Five Eyes PDF carries only the year 2026 in its imprint. The day of publication, 1 May 2026, is stated on the landing page of NCSC-NZ at https://ncsc.govt.nz/protect-your-organisation/careful-adoption-of-agentic-ai-services/.

The DSB newsletter states only its issue number 1/2026 and reports events up to 15 April 2026. The DFG guideline states the month in its form number 03/26.

The arXiv dates are those of the stored version. The first submissions were 12 February 2026 for the AGENTS.md study, 28 January 2026 for the skill formation study and 23 April 2026 for the reproduction study.

The ÖAWI guideline states only "Stand 2026" and the year of its copyright. The month, July 2026, is the publication date of the agency's own deposit on Zenodo under DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21256974. The JOHD article states its publication date, 2 March 2026, on its last page. The ReSA report states 27 May 2026 on its first page. Its attribution to the Research Software Alliance comes from the Zenodo community record of DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20321134; the PDF names only its authors and the Edinburgh workshop. The OpenSSF and CNCF guide states May 2026 as the date of version 1.0.

The NCSC-UK post of 24 March 2026 is a blog post by the NCSC's CTO for Architecture, stored as a Markdown copy of the page; it carries less weight than the NCSC's formal guidance.

The Code of Practice PDF carries no date; 10 June 2026 is the publication date of the Commission's press release at https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-publishes-code-practice-marking-and-labelling-ai-generated-content. The CNIL and CIANum note states only "juillet 2026" on its title page; the day, 20 July 2026, and the CNIL's co-authorship come from the CNIL announcement at https://cnil.fr/fr/ia-agentique-cnil-cianum-note; the PDF itself presents the note within the CIANum series on agentic AI. The BSI security information states 22.06.2026 on its first page and in every footer.

The FWF source is a news item on the FWF website. The skill formation study comes from authors affiliated with an LLM provider and tested a chat assistant.

## Excluded sources

The pages of the Austrian Datenschutzbehörde on data protection officers state no publication date and were not taken in. The FAQ of the KI-Servicestelle at RTR carries 2026 content but states no date. The OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications appeared in December 2025. The NIST summary on AI agent security (May 2026) could not be retrieved in full text. A joint opinion of EDPB and EDPS on the Digital Omnibus (January 2026) concerns the proposal stage and does not address agents. Secondary reports by law firms, news sites and vendors were excluded throughout.
