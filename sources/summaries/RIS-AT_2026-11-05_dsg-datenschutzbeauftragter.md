# Datenschutzgesetz (DSG), provisions on the data protection officer (excerpt)

Publisher: Bundeskanzleramt, Rechtsinformationssystem des Bundes (RIS)
Date: Fassung vom 05.11.2026 (last amendment listed: BGBl. I Nr. 50/2025)
Original: [RIS-AT_2026-11-05_dsg-datenschutzbeauftragter.md](../RIS-AT_2026-11-05_dsg-datenschutzbeauftragter.md)
Bindingness: Pflicht (law)
Note: Included as an approved exception to the 2026 date window, for the question of designating a data protection officer.

## Core statement

- The DSG adds no designation duty for private bodies. A text search of the whole Act found only the provisions listed here, so Art. 37 GDPR is the operative rule (Artikel 2, 1. Hauptstück, § 5).
- § 5 Abs. 1 imposes secrecy on the data protection officer and persons working for the officer, including after their activity ends.
- § 5 Abs. 2 extends a statutory right to refuse testimony to the officer, with a seizure ban for the related files.
- § 5 Abs. 3 to 5 concern the data protection officer "im öffentlichen Bereich", in particular freedom from instructions and federal ministries.
- § 57 Abs. 1 requires "jeder Verantwortliche" to designate an officer, but within the 3. Hauptstück, which under § 36 Abs. 1 covers processing by competent authorities for criminal law, public security and national security purposes.

## Relevance to the Leitfrage

### Client projects

- A client authority that is a public body under Art. 37(1)(a) GDPR needs an officer by GDPR. The DSG only adds the position rules in § 5 Abs. 3 to 5 (§ 5).
- Processing that DHCraft carries out for a university or archive under the GDPR does not fall under § 57, which is limited to criminal-law and security authorities (§ 36 Abs. 1, § 57 Abs. 1).

### Research

- Secrecy and the testimony privilege in § 5 Abs. 1 and 2 would apply to an officer DHCraft appoints voluntarily. This is an inference from the wording "Der Datenschutzbeauftragte", which names no limit to mandatory cases (§ 5 Abs. 1, 2).

### Programming

- No provision of the DSG on the data protection officer addresses software or AI tooling (whole excerpt).

### Organisation

- DHCraft's decision rests on Art. 37 GDPR, and the DSG adds secrecy duties for any officer it appoints (§ 5 Abs. 1).
- The Datengeheimnis in § 6 binds controllers, processors and their staff regardless of an officer. It lies outside the excerpt and was read only in part (§ 6 Abs. 1, 2).

## Limits

- The file is an excerpt. Other sections that mention the officer, such as the media privilege in § 9 and the Datenschutzrat composition, are not reproduced.
- RIS states the version date as the retrieval day, so earlier wording requires the amendment register.
- The classification of § 5 Abs. 1 and 2 as applying to voluntary officers in the private sector is an inference, not a statement of the text.
