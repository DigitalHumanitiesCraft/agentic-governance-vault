# Richtlinie der Österreichischen Agentur für wissenschaftliche Integrität zur guten wissenschaftlichen Praxis (Guideline on good scientific practice of the Austrian Agency for Research Integrity)

Publisher: Österreichische Agentur für wissenschaftliche Integrität (ÖAWI)
Date: 2026-07 (publication date of the Zenodo deposit, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21256974; the PDF itself states "Stand 2026" on its title page and "© 2026" in the imprint, PDF pp. 1 to 2)
Original: [OeAWI_2026-07_richtlinie-gute-wissenschaftliche-praxis.pdf](../OeAWI_2026-07_richtlinie-gute-wissenschaftliche-praxis.pdf)
Bindingness: Vorgabe for the member institutions of ÖAWI and their members, who adopted it jointly as self-regulation (PDF p. 5). It is no law; it applies "in Ergänzung und nicht in Konkurrenz zum Rechtssystem" (PDF p. 5). DHCraft is not a member, so the guideline binds DHCraft only through cooperation with member institutions or by contract; otherwise it is the Austrian reference standard for good scientific practice.

## Core statement
The 17-page guideline is the 2026 revision of the Austrian research integrity standard of 2015, written by ÖAWI and adopted jointly by its member institutions (PDF pp. 4 to 5). It covers all phases of research from the idea to publication, including proposals, peer review, supervision and consulting, in all disciplines (PDF p. 5). The revision adds explicit AI rules: responsibility for every step and result of AI use lies with the user, AI use is disclosed according to the standards of the discipline, AI may not decide in peer review and material under review may not be uploaded to insufficiently protected AI systems, and use of AI against the standards of the discipline is listed as a breach of good scientific practice (PDF pp. 6, 8, 9). Institutions must promote AI literacy (PDF pp. 10 to 11).

## Relevance to the Leitfrage
### Client projects
- Cross-sector cooperation does not lower the standard. "Wenn Forschende in sektorübergreifenden Kooperationen tätig sind, müssen sie zumindest dieselben Standards guter wissenschaftlicher Praxis einhalten wie in nicht-sektorübergreifenden Projekten." (PDF p. 8, section 3.4 c). The glossary counts companies as partners and defines cross-sector cooperation as cooperation between sectors such as companies and academia (PDF p. 14, notes 8 and 9). This makes the guideline relevant whenever DHCraft works with Austrian universities or academy institutes.
- All partners in a research project are responsible for the integrity of the project and its results (PDF p. 8, section 3.4 a).
- Researchers who supervise projects that involve third parties are responsible for communicating and enforcing the standards (PDF p. 7, section 3.1 j).
- Institutions are recommended to settle rights and duties concerning research data and results in all contracts (PDF p. 11, section 5.2).
- Candidate rule: in projects with Austrian research institutions, DHCraft applies the ÖAWI standards to its own agent use, including disclosure and documentation, and settles this in the contract.

### Research
- Responsibility: "Bei Verwendung von KI-Anwendungen liegt die Verantwortung für alle damit in Verbindung stehenden Schritte, Ergebnisse und Konsequenzen jedenfalls bei den Anwendenden." (PDF p. 6, section 3.1 g).
- Disclosure: "Forschende legen die Verwendung von Hilfsmitteln, inklusive KI-Anwendungen, entsprechend der in ihrer Fachdisziplin geltenden Standards offen." (PDF p. 6, section 3.1 h). The guideline ties disclosure to the discipline's standard and does not define a format.
- Documentation: researchers document methods and procedures of data collection and all processing steps and their results completely and comprehensibly for third parties (PDF p. 7, section 3.2 a), and keep protocols and metadata secured for a specified period (PDF p. 7, section 3.2 b; glossary note 5 on PDF p. 14 cites § 2f Abs. 3 FOG with at least ten years). Inference: for agent-performed processing steps on research data, this standard covers prompts, logs and intermediate results as processing records, although the guideline does not say so explicitly.
- Peer review: AI use in review and evaluation is disclosed, material under review may not be uploaded to insufficiently protected AI systems, and "Für die eigentliche Urteilsfindung sind KI-Anwendungen nicht zulässig." (PDF p. 8, section 3.5 a). The glossary names confidentiality, transparency, quality assurance and responsibility as the basis and refers to the DFG position of 2026 (PDF p. 15, note 10).
- Misconduct: fabrication or falsification of sources, data or results is a breach (PDF p. 9, section 4.1 a), and so is "Die Nutzung von Anwendungen künstlicher Intelligenz entgegen der in der jeweiligen Fachdisziplin geltenden Standards." (PDF p. 9, section 4.1 e). A breach becomes misconduct when committed intentionally or with gross negligence (PDF p. 9; definitions PDF p. 15).
- Authorship rests on significant scientific contributions and on approval of and responsibility for the final version (PDF p. 7, section 3.3 a). Inference: an AI system cannot meet these criteria, but the guideline does not say so explicitly.
- Candidate rule: every reference, quotation and source statement an agent produces is checked by a person before use, because invented sources count as fabrication regardless of the tool.

### Programming
The source says nothing specific on this field. Research software is not mentioned; the documentation duty for processing steps (PDF p. 7) applies to code that processes research data only by inference.

### Organisation
- Institutions ensure the continuous teaching of good scientific practice; "Dazu ist auch KI-Kompetenz zu fördern, um den kritisch reflektierten Umgang mit KI-Anwendungen zu stärken." (PDF p. 10, section 5.1). Attention is to be drawn to the risk of misconduct through misuse of AI (PDF p. 11). The glossary defines AI literacy with Art. 3 No. 56 of the AI Act (PDF p. 16, note 19).
- Persons acting as team leads or supervisors must be able to convey the standards and handle suspected misconduct (PDF p. 11, section 5.1).
- Toleration of breaches by others despite a special duty to act is itself a possible breach; the glossary names managing directors among the persons with such a duty (PDF p. 10, section 4.1 j; PDF p. 16, note 18).

## Limits
- Self-regulation of member institutions; for a company outside the membership it binds only through cooperation or contract.
- AI rules are short and principle-based. The guideline does not define what counts as an insufficiently protected AI system beyond the glossary reference to secured, non-public processing (PDF p. 15), and it defers disclosure details to the standards of each discipline.
- Agents, tool use and code are not mentioned.
- The PDF states only the year; the month comes from the Zenodo deposit of the publisher.
