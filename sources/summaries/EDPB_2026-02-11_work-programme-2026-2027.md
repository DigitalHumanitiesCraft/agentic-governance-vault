# EDPB Work Programme 2026-2027
Publisher: European Data Protection Board (EDPB)
Date: Adopted on 11 February 2026 (cover page)
Original: [EDPB_2026-02-11_work-programme-2026-2027.pdf](../EDPB_2026-02-11_work-programme-2026-2027.pdf)
Bindingness: Planning document of the EDPB. It creates no obligations for controllers or processors and states no rule on Art. 37 GDPR.

## Core statement
The work programme lists the guidance the EDPB intends to develop in 2026 and 2027. Among the planned guidance on key concepts it names a "Targeted update of the DPO Guidelines" (PDF p. 3). It does not say which points the update covers or when it will appear. It also lists joint guidelines on the interplay between the AI Act and the GDPR (PDF p. 7) and guidelines on generative AI and data scraping (PDF p. 8).

## Relevance to the Leitfrage
The document answers none of the questions on the duty to designate a data protection officer. Its only use is to show that the existing DPO guidance is under revision and that AI related guidance is announced.

### Client projects
- The existing DPO guidelines remain the reference for the criteria of Art. 37 GDPR until the announced update is published, so a decision on designation should be revisited when it appears. This is an inference from the planning entry. Location: PDF p. 3, "Targeted update of the DPO Guidelines".

### Research
- Guidelines on the processing of data for scientific research purposes are on the same list, which matters for research projects with personal data. Location: PDF p. 3.

### Programming
- Joint guidelines on the AI Act and GDPR interplay and guidelines on generative AI data scraping are announced, but not yet content. Location: PDF p. 7 and PDF p. 8.

### Organisation
- DHCraft should record the designation decision with its reasons and plan a review when the updated DPO guidelines are published. This is an inference, not a statement of the source. Location: PDF p. 3.

## Limits
The document is a plan and states no criteria, no small company rule and no AI specific rule on DPOs. The content and timing of the update are not given. The list is subject to change by the EDPB.
