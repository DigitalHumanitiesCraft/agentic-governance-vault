# AI in Funding Applications: Transparency and Accountability

Publisher: FWF Austrian Science Fund
Date: July 7, 2026 (as stated on the source)
Original: [FWF_2026-07-07_ki-in-foerderantraegen.md](../FWF_2026-07-07_ki-in-foerderantraegen.md)
Bindingness: Vorgabe (funder requirement)

## Core statement
This is a news item of the FWF on the use of AI in funding applications. AI use is generally permitted, but applicants remain solely responsible for the content, significant AI contribution to the design or drafting must be disclosed, and AI errors count as a violation of good scientific practice that the FWF sanctions.

## Relevance to the Leitfrage
### Client projects
The source says nothing specific on this field. It applies where DHCraft prepares an FWF application for a client or a partner institution, because the applicant is always responsible for the content (section "Researchers bear sole responsibility for content").

### Research
- AI use in preparing proposals is generally permitted (section "AI in Funding Applications"). "The use of artificial intelligence in the preparation of research proposals is generally permitted."
- Disclosure is required if AI tools contributed significantly to the design or drafting of a proposal; it must be explicit (lead paragraph and first section).
- Disclosure is not required for actions irrelevant to the content, named as literature reviews, language editing and translations (first section).
- The applicant is responsible for everything the application contains, regardless of the extent of AI use, and must check all information for accuracy (section "Researchers bear sole responsibility for content").
- Hallucinations, erroneous passages and inaccurate references attributable to AI have been received and count as a violation of the guidelines for good scientific practice. Sanctions can include an application ban or a demand for repayment of funding (section "Penalties for improper use of AI").
- Candidate rule: every reference and factual claim in an AI-assisted application is verified by a human against the source before submission, and significant AI contribution is disclosed.

### Programming
The source says nothing specific on this field.

### Organisation
- The FWF states that it monitors technical and legal developments and will adjust its regulations as needed (last section). DHCraft should treat the rule as changeable and re-check the FWF page before each submission.

## Limits
- A news item, not the formal guideline text. It does not define "significant", nor how or where disclosure is to be made.
- It covers applicants only, not peer review, and does not mention data protection, confidentiality or tool choice.
- The listed sanctions are named as possible ("can include"); the source gives no procedure or criteria.
